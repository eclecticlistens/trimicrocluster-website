Books brought into our conversations

Discussed · 14 Oct 2026

Curtis Roads — Microsound

The artist brought this book into the TMC percussion discussion and requested page-numbered analysis of possible applications. Microsound treats sound-particle processes and the perceptual transitions between individual events and textures. No specific proposed Roads-inspired technique is being claimed as implemented in Objects without code and listening verification.

Official MIT Press edition record ↗

First hardcover 2002; paperback 2004. Page mappings to TMC remain to be reviewed against the artist's physical copy.

Shown by artist · 23 Oct 2026

V. J. Manzo and Will Kuhn — Interactive Composition

The artist shared a photograph of the book beside handwritten AOS triangle sketches. Interactive composition is clearly relevant to the larger field, but we have not established a direct contribution from this book to any Conductor function.

Hardware / visual reference

monome grid editions

The editions show differing LED colours and brightness behaviour through the product's history. Earlier units included orange or yellow-orange LEDs, while later models used warm white or clear white. The orange of this website represents TMC's preferred visual identity; it is not presented as a calibrated hardware value.

Further reading — not adopted by default

AI-assisted musical instrument design

Hugh Aynsley et al. — “Instant Design” (NIME 2025)

Five strategies for using generative AI in workshops about new musical-interface concepts. Useful for comparing different ways creative ideas and technical proposals can be organised, but it uses text-to-image methods and should not be presented as equivalent to TMC's coding process.

Read the peer-reviewed paper ↗

Human–AI musical interaction

Sandy Ma and Charles Patrick Martin — “Touching Wires” (NIME 2025)

A tactile textile interface for human–AI co-creation. Its relevance is mainly conceptual: the human role, interaction and creative partnership, not norns-specific synthesis or TMC's temporal algorithm.
