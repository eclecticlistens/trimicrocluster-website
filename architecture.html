Architecture & Time

A functional explanation of the Conductor, temporal organisation, Anchor / Orbit / Shimmer, and where musical decisions occur.

In brief

Temporal means relating to time: when something can happen, how long it remains, when it withdraws and how relationships change. The Conductor establishes conditions; individual layers retain local musical behaviours.

Time is more than BPM

A conventional clock measures tempo, but TMC also deals with duration, silence, participation, changes of density, and the return of musical material. “Autonomous organisation of musical time” describes the Conductor's role more precisely than the earlier decorative internal phrase “Gold Temporal Field”.

The Conductor

The Conductor influences PLAY/IDLE permission for applicable sound layers and can influence larger-scale changes. It does not need to prescribe each note or gesture. Internal layer behaviours can still choose phrasing and pitch inside those conditions.

The exemption is significant: SubHarm was deliberately returned to operation outside the normal PLAY/IDLE permission gate. A public architectural description must retain that exception.

Anchor / Orbit / Shimmer

These are three roles with different musical purposes. Anchor offers a reference; Orbit creates movement in relation to material; Shimmer introduces variation, detail and emergence. They are relationship descriptions, not separate physical circuits.

The uneven triangle is based on the artist's sketches; the geometry is a communication device, not an exact numeric readout. In the grid visual design, differing brightness levels can indicate differing roles and states.

A developing pitch world

Earlier work made use of Bloom-related scales, with DPool forming a distinct low-register source. Subsequent discussions favoured removing Bloom code while retaining and evolving the microtonal approach. This is a planned architectural direction; it should not be misreported as a dependency removal proven complete for Core.

Different kinds of autonomy

Artist

Develops the musical intention, listens, tests and accepts or rejects changes.

Conductor

Organises applicable permission and larger-scale participation over time.

Layers

Produce locally constrained sounds and events, with their own sound engines and tendencies.

Development AI

Helps with code, research and documentation. It is not the autonomous musical Conductor running on norns.
