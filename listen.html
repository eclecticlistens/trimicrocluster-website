These short studies are being prepared to accompany the architecture articles. The starting point is a minute of music with a small group of layers enabled and the Conductor running in AUTO. The aim is to hear the system at work, not to turn every feature into a solo demonstration.

Recordings are not available yet. The combinations below are recording outlines. They do not describe completed performances, and no player is shown until its audio is available.

Reading an audio example

Each recording will identify its edition, enabled layers, Conductor setting and the AOS and behaviour states actually used. A layer can be enabled yet silent during part of a take, and a starting state need not remain unchanged for the full minute.

That context keeps the audio useful. The diagrams and descriptions should follow the recording rather than make a performance appear to demonstrate something it did not.

Beyond a minute

A minute is an introduction, not a full account of TMC's longer development. Extended passages can show how a relationship settles, withdraws or returns. The archive will place short studies alongside longer recordings as those pieces are selected.

Original WAVs can be retained as recording masters while smaller listening copies serve the website. Finished pieces and audiovisual studies will have their own context rather than being presented as technical tests.

About the recording outlines

These are proposed recording combinations developed from Nick's idea for one-minute WAV examples with the Conductor in AUTO. They are not historical evidence or an instruction to change the instrument's implementation. No fixed layer-to-AOS assignments or audible outcomes are claimed before a take has been heard.
