Music & Listening

Audio recordings and audiovisual studies will be presented as evidence of artistic choices, with instrument edition, date and listening context recorded.

In brief

The initial documentation build does not contain user-supplied recordings. This page outlines the archive format without inventing tracks, releases or listening links.

Why recordings matter

TriMicroCluster's behaviour cannot be judged by code or diagrams alone. The artist repeatedly used extended listening to decide whether evolving musical relationships felt convincing. The archive should make those judgements audible rather than simply describe them.

Three kinds of audio material

Listening tests

Brief examples linked to specific changes, with before-and-after examples where recordings have been retained.

Performance material

Longer works captured from Core or the specialised Advanced environment, labelled with the actual setup.

Audiovisual studies

Future graphics and live visuals can communicate relationships between the music and visual forms derived from TMC's behaviour.

Releases

Finished music will be linked to Bandcamp when approved and released. No existing release is assumed.

Context to record with each piece

Whenever possible: date, duration, Core/Advanced edition, relevant sound engines or external equipment, the particular experiment, and the final listening conclusion. If a result was edited after recording, that should also be stated.
