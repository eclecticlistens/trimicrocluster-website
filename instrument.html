The Instrument

TriMicroCluster is a generative musical instrument for norns. It explores microtonality, evolving relationships, autonomous organisation and the creative possibilities of systems that are partly directed and partly allowed to unfold.

In brief

TMC is not a fixed sequence. It uses musical roles, independent sound layers and a temporal Conductor. The latest documented eight-layer system belongs to Advanced; Core will be separately verified before public release.

A musical system rather than a fixed piece

TriMicroCluster grew from experiments in the monome norns environment, including early Bloom-derived pitch and cellular ideas. Its direction became increasingly independent: a shared vocabulary of Anchor, Orbit and Shimmer, variable relationships among layers, and an autonomous Conductor shaping participation over time.

Its aim is not to remove the musician. The performer defines the aesthetic, chooses and rejects approaches through listening, and may intervene in performance. The instrument provides a place for detail and relationships to emerge without sequencing every individual event.

Tri names the three relational roles; Micro reflects the microtonal pitch world; and Cluster describes interacting musical material. These meanings were explicitly discussed during naming in August 2026.

Eight sound layers — Advanced snapshot

The documented October 2026 arrangement consists of Drone, SubHarm, Rings Cells, three tonal voices and two arpeggio voices. These are not simply eight identical channels: they draw on different sound-generating approaches and musical roles.

Specific technologies discussed or used include Field Drone, DPool for SubHarm, MiRings for resonant Cells, sample phrases and Plaits-based arpeggiations. Engine and sample dependencies still require a release-level code and licence audit.

Still, Drift, Shine and Mix

The TRI per-layer vocabulary describes approaches to activity and change, rather than fixed instrumental categories. Earlier parameter menus also used the SDSA vocabulary of Still, Drift, Shine and Auto; interface details changed through development, so the current release menu must be checked against the code.

For a reader, the key distinction is between what a layer tends to do and when the Conductor allows it to participate. Those are related but separate questions.

What isn't a finished public download?

Core is intended as a standalone edition. Advanced includes personal hardware routing and percussive MIDI behaviours. Neither the availability of source code nor compatibility with every original norns hardware version is being assumed here.
